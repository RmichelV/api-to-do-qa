        Powerful Performance and Efficient Engines

        The Chevrolet Equinox features a responsive 1.5-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engine that produces 175 horsepower. Drivers in Hawkinsville will appreciate how effortlessly the Equinox handles daily driving demands while maintaining impressive fuel economy.

        Shoppers from Perry, GA, will find this powertrain combination ideal for their varied driving needs. The transmission's intelligent design ensures you always have the right gear for any situation, maximizing both performance and fuel efficiency.

        The Equinox offers two drivetrain configurations to suit your preferences. Front-wheel drive models achieve an EPA-rated 27 mpg combined fuel economy, with 26 mpg in the city and 29 mpg on the highway. Customers traveling from Warner Robins will appreciate the confident handling the AWD system provides during inclement weather conditions.

        Interior Comfort and Spacious Design

        Step inside the Chevrolet Equinox and discover a cabin designed with passenger comfort as the priority. The generous 40.9 inches of front legroom ensures drivers and front passengers have ample space to stretch out during longer journeys. With seating for five passengers, the Equinox comfortably accommodates the whole family.

        Cargo capacity is equally impressive, with 63.5 cubic feet of maximum storage space available when you fold down the rear seats. Families near Dublin, GA, will find the spacious interior ideal for transporting sports equipment, luggage, and everyday essentials.

        Standard features across the lineup include heated front seats and a heated steering wheel with automatic activation, ensuring comfort during cooler months. Climate control options range from single-zone air conditioning on the LT trim to dual-zone automatic climate control on RS and ACTIV models, allowing the driver and front passenger to set individual temperature preferences. The AutoSense hands-free, programmable power liftgate makes loading cargo effortless, especially when your hands are full.

        Advanced Technology and Connectivity

        The Equinox comes with an impressive 11.3-inch diagonal advanced color LCD featuring Google Built-In compatibility. This system includes navigation capability, connected apps, and Natural Voice Recognition for hands-free operation. Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto integration ensures seamless smartphone connectivity, while wireless phone charging keeps your devices powered throughout your journey.

        Remote Start allows you to warm up or cool down your Equinox before entering, while Keyless Open and Start provides effortless access. The Universal Home Remote can be programmed to operate garage doors and other compatible devices, adding convenience to your daily routine around Cochran, GA.

        Comprehensive Safety Features

        Safety is paramount in the Chevrolet Equinox, which comes standard with Chevy Safety Assist-a suite of advanced driver assistance technologies. This includes Enhanced Automatic Emergency Braking, Front Pedestrian and Bicyclist Braking, Enhanced Lane Keep Assist with Lane Departure Warning, Forward Collision Alert, Following Distance Indicator, and IntelliBeam automatic high beam control.

        Additional standard safety features include Adaptive Cruise Control for relaxed highway driving, Blind Zone Steering Assist to help prevent lane-change collisions, and Rear Cross Traffic Braking for safer backing maneuvers. Drivers from Eastman, GA, will appreciate these technologies that help make every journey safer.

        Available Trims

        • LT - The foundation of the lineup featuring cloth seating, 17-inch Grazen Metallic machined-face aluminum wheels, and single-zone air conditioning with available upgrades
        • RS - Sport-inspired styling with Evotex seat trim, 19-inch Carbon Flash Metallic machined-face aluminum wheels, and dual-zone automatic climate control
        • ACTIV - Adventure-ready design featuring Sueded Microfiber seating, 17-inch Dark Android machined-face aluminum wheels, and 235/65R17 all-terrain tires
